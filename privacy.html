Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Yoruyomi Studio ("the App") is used only by its owner. It does not collect personal information from any other person.

  1. Data accessed: With the owner's authorization, the App uses TikTok access tokens to upload videos and to read basic profile information and the performance statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) of the owner's own videos.
  2. Storage: Tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer, with file permissions restricted to the owner. They are not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.
  3. Deletion: The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok's settings, and the locally stored data is deleted together with the App's files.
  4. Contact: Questions can be raised as an issue on this repository.

プライバシーポリシー（日本語）

本アプリは運営者本人のみが使用し、他者の個人情報は収集しません。本人の許可のもと、動画の投稿と、本人の動画の基本情報・実績（再生・いいね・コメント・シェア数）の取得のみを行います。認証情報と実績データは本人のコンピューター内にのみ保存し、第三者へ提供しません。
